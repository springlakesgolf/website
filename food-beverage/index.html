The Clubhouse

A table at the club

The clubhouse dining room and patio are a private setting for members and their guests. The kitchen blends classic flavours with a few creative twists, and the patio looks out over the moraine for long evenings in the sun.

Dining is reserved for members. If you would like to enjoy the clubhouse without a golf membership, a social membership is the way in. Get in touch and we will share the details.

To reserve a table, call the clubhouse at 905-640-3633.
