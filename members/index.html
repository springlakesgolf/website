Club Policies

Regulations & Guidelines

These policies keep a positive, safe club atmosphere. For safety and liability reasons, “spectators” and “walk/ride-along” guests are not permitted.

Golf Course Care

  • Repair all ball marks on greens.
  • Replace and pat down all divots.
  • Dispose of all garbage into the garbage cans.
  • Use care when replacing flags.
  • No more than one practice swing on the tee.
  • Don’t pry the ball out of the cup with a club head.
  • Rake sand traps and place (don’t throw) the rake in the trap.

Dress Code

  • Not permitted: short shorts/skirts, cut-offs, leggings, t-shirts, exposed midriff, tank & halter tops, sweatpants, or caps worn backward.
  • Jeans may be worn in the clubhouse, but not on the course, putting green, range or pitching area.
  • Gentlemen’s shirts must have sleeves and collars (turtlenecks fine) and stay tucked in.
  • Men’s hats face forward and are removed in clubhouse dining areas.

Practice Facility

  • No practising or hitting extra balls on fairways, bunkers, greens or clubhouse lawns.
  • Confine practice to designated areas; range balls are for the range & chipping area only.
  • Use your own balls on the putting green.
  • In poor weather or early-finish nights, the range & practice greens won’t stay open late.
  • Range closed until noon Mondays (or Tuesdays after a holiday) for maintenance.

Etiquette

  • Play “Ready Golf” and keep pace with the group ahead.
  • All buildings are non-smoking, which also applies to the Pro Shop porch and patios.
  • A member’s spouse is a guest and has no membership privileges.
  • Refer to the annual renewal forms for pricing and yearly rules.

Guests

  • Guests are the responsibility of the member.
  • Junior memberships are for golfers 18 and under and must be linked to an adult membership. Junior tee times start from 11 am and juniors may not use carts, unless approved by management.
  • Junior and junior intermediate members may bring up to two guests per visit, unless approved by management.
  • Guest rates are set before the season; see the annual renewal form.

Penalties

  • Not checking in before a round results in immediate suspension.
  • Not paying for your cart leads to suspension and loss of cart privileges.
  • Playing extra holes or rounds without payment leads to suspension.
  • Repeated offences lead to removal from the club.
