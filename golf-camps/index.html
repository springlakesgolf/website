About Our Camps

A summer on the moraine

Each season our professionals run junior camps that grow young golfers’ skills and love of the game in a fun, supportive environment.

All Ages & Abilities

Programs designed for beginners through to keen young competitors, grouped by stage.

Skills That Last

Full-swing, short game, putting and on-course play, all taught by PGA of Canada professionals.

Fun First

Games, challenges and time on three courses make camp the highlight of a junior’s summer.

Looking for Year-Round Junior Golf?

Beyond summer camps, our academy runs junior development programs and a performance pathway all season long.
