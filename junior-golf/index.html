Three Programs

A pathway for young golfers

Summer camp, Operation 36 and the junior academy, all taught by our PGA of Canada professionals.

Junior Camps

Ages 6 to 14, July and August

Four weeks of summer camp, each a full week of skills, games and time on the course with our professionals.

Camp details

Operation 36

Sign-up opens in February

Our development program for newer junior golfers. Weekly coaching with our professionals, plus nine-hole rounds on the East Course that start 25 yards from the green. Shoot 36 or better and you move back to the next distance.

Ask about Operation 36

Junior Academy

Applications open in February

Our performance program for committed juniors ready to compete. Weekly guided practice, on-course coaching on the East Course, and a path into junior tournaments. By application, so we can group players by level.

Apply to the academy

Looking for Private Lessons?

Our professionals also teach juniors one on one, booked online through CoachNow.
